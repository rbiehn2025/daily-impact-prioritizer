Summary

Executive summary

Partial Platform Health Assessment for Boll & Branch (project 4383). This pass adds Sigma campaign and volume trends (6,276 total campaigns; 1,827 Running; email-only sends) on top of Journeys, Lists, Experiments, Snippets, and profile/event trend CSVs. Campaign audit, custom-event-usage, profile field usage counts, and template handlebars are still missing.

Top operational risks: 1,033 Running experiments (586 pre-2023) and 1,827 Running campaigns (29%). Journey enablement is 52.4% (267/510) with 56 enabled journeys showing orphaned nodes. List inventory: 1,546 dynamic; 315 Contains / 45 MatchesRegex.

Send volume is email-only and seasonal (~229M emails in 2024; peak Dec 2025 ~68.6M). Profiles grew ~3.17M to ~4.75M (Feb 2024–Sep 2026). Custom events spiked to ~10.0M in Jan 2026 without per-event usage data to explain the shift.
